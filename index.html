Makes a fresh random scramble each time. Press play to watch it applied to a solved cube, at the turn time you chose.

Orientation

Each orientation gives the same pattern in different colours. Turns are always read from this hold.

Moves

Letters name the face to turn clockwise. A prime (') turns it counter-clockwise, and 2 is a half turn. M, E and S turn the middle slices; x, y and z rotate the whole cube. Tap a move to jump to that point.
